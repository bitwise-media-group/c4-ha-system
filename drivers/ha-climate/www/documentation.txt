Home Assistant Climate (Bitwise Media Group)

Represents one Home Assistant climate entity (thermostat, heat pump, air
conditioner, ...) as a Control4 thermostat.

Requires the Home Assistant Gateway driver in the project. The driver binds to
the gateway automatically.

Setup
1. Add one Home Assistant Climate driver per climate entity.
2. While the gateway is connected, pick the entity from the Entity Selector
   list, or type the entity_id (e.g. climate.living_room) into Entity ID.
3. In Composer, bind the room to its thermostat: in the room's bindings
   (Connections > Room Bindings), set the room's Temperature and Temperature
   Control to this driver's Room Selection connection. The room's climate
   (Comfort) tile then tracks this thermostat live. Without the binding the
   tile shows a generic icon with no state.

The driver deliberately does not auto-bind Room Selection: Control4
auto-binding attaches every still-unbound room in the project to the driver
being added, which cross-wires room tiles to the wrong thermostats when
several rooms and drivers are involved. Bind each room explicitly.

Upgrading from a version that auto-bound Room Selection: check each room's
Temperature / Temperature Control bindings and repoint any that reference the
wrong thermostat at the room's own one.

The driver adapts to the entity's capabilities from Home Assistant:

- HVAC modes follow the entity. Home Assistant's heat_cool (or auto with a
  target range) appears as Auto in Control4 with separate heat and cool
  setpoints; heat/cool/dry use a single setpoint; dry shows as Dry and
  fan_only as Fan. If an entity offers both auto and heat_cool, only
  heat_cool is reachable as Auto.
- Fan modes appear when the entity has them, using Home Assistant's mode
  names verbatim.
- Current humidity is shown when the entity reports it.
- Home Assistant presets (eco, away, boost, ...) appear under Extras.
- Setpoint limits and step follow the entity's min/max and step.

Temperatures follow the Home Assistant server's configured unit
automatically; no unit setup is needed.

Not supported: hold modes, humidity setpoints, and thermostat scheduling
(use Home Assistant automations instead).
